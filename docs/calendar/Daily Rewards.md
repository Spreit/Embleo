### Original Calendar Reward Rules
Today's reward can be claimed for free. Claiming rewards from previous days requires 1,000 Gald.
Regular day prizes cycle between 5 PB-stones and 5,000 Gold.
Wednesdays have episode releases and gift 30 gems. (did double episode release give 60?)
Character birthdays award 100 gems.
Raid events start on fridays and last until Wednesday (episode release)

If a calendar event falls onto a regular day that awards gems:
- 5 PB-stone reward is replaced with the higher amount (like, 5 is replaced with 100), NOT stacked.
